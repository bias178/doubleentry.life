Same development spend, a different margin.

Illustrative case / round numbers

Two companies spend 100 on development this year. Same cash out, same product.

Company A expenses it. The full 100 hits operating costs.

Company B meets the IAS 38 criteria and capitalizes it. The 100 goes on the balance sheet and reaches the P&L as amortization over the following years.
