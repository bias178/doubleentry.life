The dividend never appears in the income statement.

Illustrative case / round numbers

A company earns 100 million euro and pays a dividend of 40 million on its ordinary shares.

The income statement shows 100. The dividend never appears in it, because it is a distribution of profit and not an expense. It is debited directly to equity: retained earnings fall by 40 when the dividend is declared, and cash falls by 40 when it is paid.

Million euroIncome statementRetained earningsCash
Profit for the year100+100
Dividend declared-40
Dividend paid-40
Net effect100+60-40

Illustrative case, round numbers. Cash from the profit itself is not shown.

The income statement stops at profit. What happens to the profit is recorded in equity.
